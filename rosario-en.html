A journey back to the essence. A journey of body, energy, and consciousness. My journey into the world of wellness began in Austria. In 1997, I graduated from a specialized school in Walchsee, where I trained in relaxation massage, reflexology, lymphatic drainage, and deep-tissue massage.

Rosario Lill Herrle

For three years, I worked at the Vital Royal Hotel in Seefeld under the supervision of doctors who took a holistic approach. This experience taught me to view the body as a whole and, above all, to listen to each person beyond their symptoms. My journey led me to Thailand, where I delved deeper into Thai massage.

In 2000, I met my Reiki teacher, Despina Tzatarma—an encounter that opened a new dimension on my path of consciousness and energy. Between 2000 and 2008, I lived in Italy, near Lake Garda, where my children, Leo (2004) and Gabri (2007), were born. In 2003, I traveled to Kerala, India, where I was introduced to Ayurveda and an ancient vision of balance between body, mind, and nature.

In 2002, I received my second-degree Reiki certification in Puerto Viejo, a place that would eventually become my home. In 2015, I graduated from a School of Metaphysics in Puerto Viejo, deepening my study of consciousness, energy, and spirituality. Over the years, I continued to expand my training, including craniosacral therapy with Dan Springer. In 2021, I became certified as a Reiki Master with Julie Hickey.

Today, I integrate all of this journey into my work through massage, reflexology, lymphatic drainage, deep tissue massage, Thai massage, craniosacral therapy, Reiki, Ayurveda, metaphysics, and sound healing. But beyond the techniques, my work stems from presence, intuition, and listening.

I believe that the body has its own language. When we create a safe space to stop, breathe and listen to ourselves, we can begin to reconnect with our own capacity for balance and well-being. After almost three decades of learning, I feel that my greatest teacher has been life itself: every place, every encounter, every experience and every person that has been part of this path.
