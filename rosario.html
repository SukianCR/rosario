A journey back to one's essence. A path involving body, energy, and consciousness. My journey in the wellness world began in Austria. In 1997, I graduated from a specialized school in Walchsee, where I trained in relaxation massage, reflexology, lymphatic drainage, and deep tissue massage.

For three years, I worked at the Hotel Vital Royal in Seefeld under the supervision of doctors with a holistic approach. This experience taught me to view the body as a whole and, above all, to listen to each person beyond their symptoms. My journey led me to Thailand, where I deepened my practice of Thai massage. In 2000, I met my Reiki teacher, Despina Tzatarma—an encounter that opened a new dimension in my path of consciousness and energy. Between 2000 and 2008, I lived in Italy near Lake Garda, where my children, Leo (2004) and Gabri (2007), were born. In 2003, I traveled to Kerala, India, where I was introduced to Ayurveda and an ancient perspective on the balance between body, mind, and nature. In 2002, I received my Reiki Level II certification in Puerto Viejo—a place that would eventually become my home. In 2015, I graduated from a School of Metaphysics in Puerto Viejo, further deepening my study of consciousness, energy, and spirituality. Over the years, I continued to expand my training, including craniosacral therapy with Dan Springer. In 2021, I became a certified Reiki Master under Julie Hickey. Today, I integrate this entire journey into my work, combining massage, reflexology, lymphatic drainage, deep tissue massage, Thai massage, craniosacral therapy, Reiki, Ayurveda, metaphysics, and sound healing. Yet, beyond the techniques themselves, my work is rooted in presence, intuition, and deep listening. I believe the body has its own language. When we create a safe space to pause, breathe, and listen to ourselves, we can begin to reconnect with our innate capacity for balance and well-being. After nearly three decades of learning, I feel that life itself has been my greatest teacher—encompassing every place, encounter, experience, and person that has been part of this path.
